How Doubly Linked List Works:

  • PREV pointer: Points to previous booking (navigate backward ←)
  • NEXT pointer: Points to next booking (navigate forward →)
  • Bidirectional: Can traverse both ways unlike singly linked list
  • Operations: Insert O(1), Delete O(1), Search O(n)
